Support

Email dustinlrotstein@gmail.com. We aim to reply within one business day.

To help us help you, include:

  • your Jira site address (for example yourcompany.atlassian.net),
  • what you did and what you expected to happen,
  • a screenshot, if you can.

Please don't send passwords, API tokens or other secrets.

Common questions

I can see the log but can't add items. Logging and editing items needs Jira's "Edit issues" permission in that project. Ask your Jira admin.

I can't delete an item. Only project admins, or the person who logged the item, can delete it.

Where is the portfolio? In Jira's left sidebar, under Apps → RAIDflow portfolio.
