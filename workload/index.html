Workload: Team Capacity & Overbooking Alerts for Jira

See who's overbooked next week and fix it in one click. No setup, no new plan to maintain.

  • Workload from the Jira you already have: each person's load, week by week, from estimates, story points and due dates, including their work in other projects.
  • One-click fixes: hand an issue to a teammate with room, or push it to a later week. Workload uses your own Jira permissions.
  • A Monday alert, not another dashboard: a comment on an issue you choose, @mentioning anyone who's overbooked.
  • Working weeks that match reality: hours per week, working days, time off, and US, UK and Canadian public holidays.
  • Runs on Atlassian: your data stays in Atlassian's cloud.

How the load is worked out

Workload counts each person's open, assigned issues. Hours come from the remaining estimate, then the original estimate, then story points, then a default you set. Work with only a due date lands on the last working days before it. A week is overbooked when the load goes over your threshold (100% by default).

Limits

The grid reads up to 3,000 open issues and says so when it stops. Issues with no estimate use your default hours, so numbers are most accurate when issues are estimated.
