To help us help you, include:

  • your Jira site address (for example yourcompany.atlassian.net),
  • what you did and what you expected to happen,

Common questions

The grid is empty. Workload counts open issues that have an assignee. Assign issues, and add due dates and estimates, then click Refresh.

Everyone looks lightly loaded. Issues with no estimate count as the team's default hours (4 by default). Add original or remaining estimates, or story points, for accurate numbers.

I can't change someone's working week. People edit their own. Project admins can edit anyone's on a project page, and Jira admins on the site-wide page.

The Monday alert didn't post. Open the project's Workload page, then Monday alert. It shows the last error and lets you post one now. Check that the chosen issue is in the same project and still exists.

Where is Workload? In a project's sidebar as Workload, and under Apps as Workload – everyone.
