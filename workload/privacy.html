Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

This policy explains what the Workload app for Jira Cloud ("Workload", "we") stores and how it is used. Workload is built on Atlassian Forge and runs entirely on Atlassian's cloud infrastructure ("Runs on Atlassian").

What Workload stores

  • Each person's working week, keyed by Atlassian account ID: hours per week, working days, public-holiday country, and time-off date ranges with an optional short note.
  • Team settings per project: hours per story point, default hours, the overbooking threshold, and the Monday alert settings (the issue key, weekday, the account ID of the admin who set it up, and the last date it was sent).

Workload reads issue data from Jira each time you use it and does not store it. Workload does not store passwords, payment details, email addresses or any data from outside your Jira site.

Where it is stored

All data is kept in Atlassian Forge storage, inside Atlassian's cloud, for the Jira site where Workload is installed. Workload has no servers of its own, uses no AI services and sends no data to any third party.

Who can see it

Workload follows Jira's permissions. A person sees only issues they can browse in Jira. People edit their own working week; project admins manage a team's settings and anyone's working pattern on that project, and Jira admins do so on the site-wide page. The Monday alert is posted as a Jira comment on an issue in the same project, so it is visible to people who can see that issue. We (the app's developer) have no access to your data.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until you change it or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app's stored data according to Atlassian's Forge data retention policies. A weekly job reports the account IDs Workload stores to Atlassian's personal data reporting service. When an Atlassian user closes their account, Workload deletes that person's working-week settings and anonymises their name on any alert they set up.

Your rights

To ask about, export or delete personal data, contact your Jira site admin or email us at the address below.
