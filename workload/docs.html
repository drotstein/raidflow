Workload: Team Capacity & Overbooking Alerts for Jira

Where to find it

Open a project, then choose Workload in the project sidebar. A site-wide page, Workload – everyone, is under Apps in the Jira navigation.

How the load is worked out

  • Which issues: open (not Done), assigned issues. A project's team is everyone assigned open work in it. Each person's load counts their work in all projects you can see.
  • Hours per issue: the remaining estimate, else the original estimate, else story points times the hours per point, else a default number of hours. All of these are set per team under Settings.
  • Where it lands: work with a start date is spread over its window from today. Work with only a due date lands on the last working days before it. Overdue work counts today. Undated work is listed separately and isn't counted in a week.
  • Capacity: each person's hours per week, spread over their working days, minus public holidays (US, UK England & Wales, Canada federal) and time off. This week only counts the days left.
  • Overbooked: a week is overbooked when the load goes over the team's threshold, 100% by default.

One-click fixes

For each overbooked person-week, Workload suggests one move: hand an issue to the least-loaded teammate who has room, or push its due date to the first later week with room. Apply runs as you, so Jira's own permissions decide whether the change is allowed.

Working week and time off

Choose My working week to set your hours per week, working days, public-holiday country and time off. People edit their own. Project admins can edit anyone's on a project page, and Jira admins on the site-wide page.

Monday alert

A project admin chooses Monday alert, picks an issue and a weekday, and turns it on. Each week Workload comments on that issue with who is overbooked this week and next and the suggested moves, @mentioning the people involved. The alert names only issues from its own project. Post one now sends it immediately.

Team settings

Under Settings: hours per story point, default hours for issues with no estimate, and the overbooking threshold.

Limits

  • The grid reads up to 3,000 open issues and says so when it stops.
  • Issues with no estimate use the team's default hours, so numbers are most accurate when issues are estimated.
