Getting started

Find it

Open any Jira project and choose RAIDflow in the project's tabs (it may be under More). The cross-project view is in the left sidebar under Apps → RAIDflow portfolio.

Log an item

Pick a tab (Risks, Assumptions, Issues, Dependencies, Questions, Decisions or Parking lot) and click Log. Each item gets a reference such as R-001 or DEC-002. Risks have likelihood × impact (1–5 each): a score of 15 or more is high, 5–12 medium, 1–4 low.

Turn a question into a decision

Open a question and choose Move to decisions or Move to parking lot. The answer comes along with it.

Import your workbook

Click Import workbook and choose an .xlsx file: either one tab per log ("Risks", "Issues", "Decision log"…) or one "RAID log" tab with a Type column. Columns are matched by their headers, owners are matched to Jira people by name or email, and importing the same file again adds nothing twice.

Weekly status report

Click Status report. RAIDflow reads the week's log and writes an overall status, top risks, decisions and asks. Use Copy as Markdown to paste it into Confluence, Slack or email, and check it before you send it.

Export

Export CSV saves the current view, ready for a spreadsheet or a steering pack.
