Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

This policy explains what the RAIDflow app for Jira Cloud ("RAIDflow", "we") stores and how it is used. RAIDflow is built on Atlassian Forge and runs entirely on Atlassian's cloud infrastructure ("Runs on Atlassian").

What RAIDflow stores

  • The RAID log content you enter or import: item titles, descriptions, plans, outcomes, categories, dates, statuses, scores, links to documents, and linked Jira issue keys.
  • People on items: the Atlassian account ID and display name of an item's owner, and the account ID of the person who logged it. Names imported from a spreadsheet that don't match a Jira user are kept as the text you provided.

RAIDflow does not store passwords, payment details, email addresses or any data from outside your Jira site.

Where it is stored

All data is kept in Atlassian Forge storage, inside Atlassian's cloud, for the Jira site where RAIDflow is installed. RAIDflow has no servers of its own and sends no data to any third party.

AI status reports

When someone chooses "Status report", RAIDflow sends a summary of that project's RAID log to Atlassian's Forge LLMs service, which runs AI models within Atlassian's infrastructure. It happens only on request. RAIDflow does not store the prompts and does not use your data to train models.

Who can see it

RAIDflow follows Jira's permissions. A person can see a project's log only if they can browse that project in Jira, and the portfolio shows only projects they can see. We (the app's developer) have no access to your log content.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until you delete items in RAIDflow or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes the app's stored data according to Atlassian's Forge data retention policies. When an Atlassian user closes their account, RAIDflow removes or anonymises the personal data it holds about them, as Atlassian's personal data reporting requires.

Your rights

To ask about, export or delete personal data, contact your Jira site admin or email us at the address below. Admins can export any log to CSV at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and describe the change on this page.
